Proximity-first
Discovery mechanic: where you are. The wallet opens on partner brands ranked by distance, and Discover is a single list grouped “at this terminal / within 300 m / in this city”.
Nav: floating + for add-to-wallet, ⋮ for transactions and card management.
Density: medium — one row per brand, rate always visible.
Best when the Nayax terminal estate is the moat: nothing else can rank a marketplace by the machine you are standing at.
What changed from the mockup
Cashback became the product
The mockup treated rewards as two small stat cards. Here the balance is the first thing on screen and every brand carries a rate, because partner-funded cashback is the thing Apple and Google Wallet cannot do.
“Your Apps” became mini-apps
An icon grid that sends people to the App Store is a dead end. Brands now open in-wallet with a rate, a perk list and one action — no download, no account.
The offer explains itself
No Face ID theatre. The takeover says which terminal detected you, what you get, and that it applies automatically at payment — plus a per-brand mute.
Vouchers stopped being a wall
Split into discounts and store credit, expiry surfaced as a chip, and the promise stated once: most apply at the terminal automatically instead of being shown.

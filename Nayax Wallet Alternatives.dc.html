Turn 2 · Alternatives
Three takes on the same wallet
Same content and the same partner-cashback engine — different answers to “what is this app for the first two seconds after it opens”. All three are tappable at 390 × 844. 2a is the design we've been refining; 2b and 2c are new directions.
2a
Card-first (current)
The card is the hero; a single rotating stat line under it carries cashback, savings and miles. Two tabs split suggestions from proximity, a floating + owns add-to-wallet and ⋮ owns transactions.
Strength: feels like a wallet first, marketplace second.
Risk: discovery lives below the fold.
2b
Dark vault
Near-black surface, yellow reserved for money and offers. Earnings are the headline above the card; brands are quiet dark rows with the rate as the only bright thing. Everything opens as a bottom sheet — no page changes.
Strength: premium, and cashback reads instantly.
Risk: a dark payments app is a bigger brand commitment.
2c
Pay-first rail
Opens on the two things people actually do: Tap to pay and Scan, in a yellow action band. Cards shrink to a swipeable rail, the three numbers sit in a row, and the marketplace becomes a filterable list with a real bottom nav.
Strength: fastest to a payment, most room for partners.
Risk: denser, and the card art loses its moment.
How to choose
If the wallet's job is to make people feel their Nayax card is worth carrying, take 2b. If it's to get someone through a terminal in two taps and monetise the partner network, take 2c. 2a sits between them and is the safest first release. They also mix: 2c's action band on top of 2a's card, or 2b's sheet-only navigation in any of them.
